Color.

Nine brand colors, six surfaces, eight text roles, five lines and one focus ring. The palette is Pastel: Blue and Green are soft and carry shapes, not words. Text is always Deep Teal, Teal-deep, Slate, Blue-dark or Paper.

Figma: color swatches 2719-4672

Brand palette.

Deep Teal

#264653 · --color-brand-deep-teal

Primary ink. Body text, links, Dark button, logo, dark sections, focus ring.

Do use it for body text and the Dark button.

Don't place two Deep Teal bands next to each other.

Teal-deep

#1C3742 · --color-brand-teal-deep

Darkest ink. Headlines on light surfaces and labels on Yellow.

Do use it for headlines and the Yellow button label.

Don't use it as a section background.

Teal Light

#5F7075 · --color-brand-teal-light

Muted teal for shapes and secondary surfaces.

Do use it for a kit piece or a quiet surface.

Don't use it as a text role. Slate covers secondary text.

Blue (Pastel)

#6E8B97 · --color-brand-blue

Shapes, surfaces and icons.

Do use it for kit pieces and icons.

Don't put text or a button label on it (Paper on Blue is 3.53:1). Use Blue-dark.

Blue-dark

#4E6873 · --color-brand-blue-dark

Hover fill of the Yellow button and Outline Light on photos. Secondary text on Mist. Hero kit quarter.

Do use it for every Blue fill that carries a label (Paper label 5.76:1).

Don't use it as a large section background.

Green (Pastel)

#6A8670 · --color-brand-green

Shapes, in-range markers and icons.

Do use it for an in-range marker or a confirmed icon.

Don't set text in it (3.89:1 on Paper).

Yellow

#FFBE0B · --color-brand-yellow

The one accent: a CTA, a hover or a step marker.

Do keep it to one Yellow element per viewport.

Don't use it as a section background or as text.

Coral

#E76F51 · --color-brand-coral

Errors and out-of-range values only, always with an icon.

Do use it for an error icon and the error field border.

Don't use it as a text color or as decoration.

Clay

#CFC6B8 · --color-brand-clay

Packaging and posters only.

Do use it on labels, boxes and printed posters.

Don't use it anywhere in the website UI.

Surfaces.

Paper is the page. Mist is the alternate band. Card is for raised panels. Washes are soft bands; Wash Blue and Wash Yellow count as accent surfaces for the button hover rule.

Paper

#FEFCF6 · --color-surface-paper

The page background on every screen.

Don't replace it with pure white.

Mist

#E8E7E1 · --color-surface-mist

Alternate band and hero ground.

Do use Blue-dark, not Slate, for secondary text on Mist.

Card

#FFFFFF · --color-surface-card

Dropdown panels, fields and raised cards.

Don't use it as a page or band background.

Wash Blue

The Yellow rule.

Do

Use Yellow for a CTA, a hover or a step marker, with at most one Yellow element per viewport. The Yellow button hovers to Blue-dark #4E6873 with a Paper label (5.76:1).

Don't

Use Yellow as a section background, as text, for icons, or as a label marker. Never put a Paper label on Pastel Blue #6E8B97; that color is for shapes and surfaces only.

Coral and Clay.

Coral is a state, not a color scheme.

Errors and out-of-range values only. It always comes with an icon and never colors text: the message stays Deep Teal, the icon and the field border turn Coral.

Clay is for print.

Packaging and posters only. Clay never appears in the website UI, not as a band, a card or a logo background.

Type rules.

Serif floor: 24px desktop, 21px mobile.

DM Serif Display never goes below Card serif. Anything smaller is Inter. The wordmark is artwork, not type.

Inter never above 26px.

The largest Inter style is Lead at 20px. Big numbers and headlines are DM Serif.

Hero home only on the homepage.

70/60 belongs to the homepage hero. Every other hero is 46/54.

Sentence case everywhere.

Headlines, buttons, nav items and labels. Headlines end with a full stop and are never questions. Assessment questions and picker prompts may be questions, set in H3.

Labels: 12/16 SemiBold, +2%.

Mobile 11/16. Sentence case, Slate on Paper. No Yellow marker and no uppercase.

No italic highlight.

Figma sets “yourself” upright in the hero. The serif italic highlight is not used in Edition 02. Avi may re-open it.

Line length about 68ch.

Running text stays near 68 characters per line. Headlines may run wider.

Numerals carry their unit.

A number never appears bare: “$25”, “30 minutes”, “2 tablets”. Tables and prices use tabular figures (font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums).

No letterspacing except the label.

Every heading and body style has 0 tracking. Only labels take +2%.

US English.

“Color” in site copy, “center”, “license”. Provider, assessment and member, never doctor, quiz or patient.

Elevation.

The UI is flat. Cards and panels are separated by borders, not shadows. There is one shadow, and it belongs to things that float over the page: dropdowns and dialogs.
