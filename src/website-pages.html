Current · R-85, U-28

Product colours mark their product wherever it is relevant on the website (O-03 closed): the product card (a dot or an edge), the product page accent, the product chip in menus and the recommendation card. They mark, they never carry text or data (text stays in the text colours) and never replace the category colour of the page (R-117). The product name always sits beside the colour, so colour is never the only key.
