Hashtags and links.

Three to five hashtags at the end of the caption, branded or neutral (#unifiedmeds, #menshealth, #telehealth). Never a condition, symptom or medicine. Links do not work in captions: the bio link and the story link sticker carry them. Threads: one topic tag per post at most.

Rules for this channel.

On top of the shared social rules and the rules on Voice and compliance.

  1. Plan the grid three posts at a time. Pick one rhythm (A checkerboard or B rows) and keep it; every post keeps its type inside the 3:4 grid crop.
  2. Captions carry the legal line. Wherever a post points to the assessment, "Assessment only. Not a diagnosis." appears in the caption, and on the last carousel card.
  3. Stories keep the edges clear. Top and bottom 14% stay free of type; the link sticker sits above the reply bar.
  4. Highlight covers carry icons, not words. The label is typed in the app. Five highlights, in this order: Start, How it works, Privacy, Questions, Delivery.
  5. Threads is text first. An image card only when one line deserves it; same avatar as Instagram.
