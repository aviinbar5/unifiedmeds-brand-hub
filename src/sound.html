System

Sonic branding.

How unifiedmeds sounds: the short sting sound, the few sounds the product may make, and sound in videos. Quiet by design. A member may be on a train, at work or at home with family, so the brand never makes a sound he did not ask for, and no sound says what he is treated for.

Proposed

Every sound file on this page is a prototype, synthesised in code from the notes of the approved logo stings. It shows the idea, length and level. A sound designer produces the final files before anything ships. No sound rule is in the rulebook yet; once Avi approves this page, its principles become numbered rules. The stings themselves are on Videos; every file here is also on Downloads.

Principles.

Sound follows the motion principle: "Motion confirms, it never performs" (R-49).

Sound confirms.

A sound marks something that happened: an order placed, a message arrived. It is never decoration and never runs under a screen.

Calm and warm.

Soft bell and sine tones in C major, a gentle attack, a short tail. No buzzers, alarms or bright synth leads.

Short.

The sting sound stays under 2 seconds. A product sound stays under 1 second.

Private.

Nothing hints at a condition or a body. Nothing is loud or distinctive enough to make the people nearby look up.

Always optional.

The product is silent by default. On the web nothing plays with sound until the visitor presses play.

Captions always.

Every spoken word is captioned. Every video makes sense with the sound off.

The sting sound.

The three approved logo stings (R-101, Videos) are 8 seconds long and carry their own sound. The sting sound is their short form, under 2 seconds, for places too short for a full sting: the end of a 6-second social cut, an audio ad, a podcast read. All three versions share one signature: a soft click as the two halves meet, then two rising bell notes, G and the C above it, over a quiet C major chord.

B · Two halves

Two soft rushes of air from both sides, the click and a low, soft thump as the halves meet, then the two notes. The default.

Pairs with sting B. 1.8 seconds. Listen in Files.

C1 · From u

A soft tone rises as the u turns on its hinge, a lighter click as it closes, then the two notes. For brand and story moments.

Pairs with sting C1. 1.85 seconds. Listen in Files.

C2 · From u + m

Two quiet notes for the u and the m, a short rush as they turn, the click, then the two notes. For short social openers.

Pairs with sting C2. 1.9 seconds. Listen in Files.

Times are seconds from the start of each file. Notes are the same as in the 8-second stings, so the short and long forms belong together.

Product sounds.

The product is silent by default. These three sounds exist for members who turn sounds on in the app, and nowhere else.

Silent by default.

The app and the website make no sound unless the member turns sounds on in settings. One switch covers all of them.

Push notifications use the system sound.

A push uses the phone's default sound and vibration. A unifiedmeds sound plays only if the member chooses it, because a sound people learn to recognise tells them which app it is.

Never a sound that tells.

No sound, file name or notification text names a condition, a medicine or a body part. File names stay neutral: confirm, message, error.

Never in the assessment or checkout.

No sound during the assessment, at payment or on any web page. Errors in a form are shown, never heard.

Confirm

The brand's two notes, small and fast: G then C. After an action the member took: order placed, refill confirmed.

0.55 seconds. In app only, when sounds are on.

Message from your provider

An open, unhurried C then G with a soft tail. A new message from the care team while the app is open, or as the chosen push sound.

0.95 seconds. The only sound offered for push.

Gentle error

A soft falling step, D to C, sine only and quiet. Something did not go through: a payment or an upload.

0.5 seconds. Never a buzz, never repeated.

Files.

WAV, 44.1 kHz, mono, 16-bit, peak at about -3 dBFS, with short fades. The players load nothing until you press play. The code that makes them, build_sounds.py, is the brief for the sound designer.

Sound in videos.

Music, voice and level for every video the brand makes. Silence is allowed: a video with no music at all is on brand.

Music

  • Tempo. 70 to 96 beats per minute. Steady, never a build to a drop.
  • Instruments. Felt or upright piano, soft electric piano, nylon or warm acoustic guitar, round bass, brushed or light hand percussion, soft pads, the brand bells.
  • Key. Major keys; C major where the sting sound follows, so it lands in key.
  • Level. Music sits well under the voice, about 12 dB lower while anyone speaks.
  • Rights. Licensed for every channel and the full term, or commissioned. No trending audio in paid ads.
SteadyWarmUnhurriedAssuredDiscreetAdult

Voice

  • Who. An adult voice that sounds 35 to 60, warm, mid to low, neutral US accent. One voice per piece.
  • Pace. About 140 to 160 words per minute: conversational, plain, the way a good provider explains.
  • Tone. Never whispered, never suggestive, never a hard sell. No innuendo, ever.
  • Legal lines. Any safety or legal line is read at the same pace and level as the rest. Never sped up, never pushed under music.
  • Words. The content rules apply to the script: provider, assessment, member; no claims, no numbers without units (R-56 to R-62).

Captions.

Burned in on social; a caption file (WebVTT) on the web. Inter, sentence case, at most two lines, inside the safe area, on a plain Teal-deep or Paper box so they pass AA on any frame.

The sting.

One sting per video, at the start or the end, never in the middle (R-101). The sting sound plays only with the logo on screen, never on its own under other pictures.

Do and don't.

Do: stay silent until asked.

Sound starts only when the member presses play or turns sounds on.

Don't: play on load or on hover.

No sound on page load, on scroll, on hover or behind a pop-up.

Do: use the system sound for push.

The phone's default sound keeps a notification anonymous.

Don't: ship a branded push sound by default.

A sound people learn to recognise names the app to everyone in the room.

Do: land the sting at the start or the end.

One sting per video, with the logo on screen.

Don't: loop or repeat the sting.

Never in the middle, never twice, never as a music bed.

Do: caption every word.

The story works with the sound off.

Don't: carry meaning in sound alone.

No message, warning or price that only the voice says.

Do: leave room for silence.

A pause, room tone or no music at all is on brand.

Don't: hype or hint.

No risers, drops, heartbeat or monitor sounds, sultry saxophone, whispers or innuendo.
