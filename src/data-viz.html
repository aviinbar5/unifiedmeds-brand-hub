Principles.

Eight rules that hold for every chart, tile and number in the portal.

One question per chart.

The title gives the answer in a plain sentence ("Your next refill is in 12 days"). The subtitle says what is measured and in what unit.

Unit and range, always.

A number never stands alone: it carries its unit, and a result carries its range (R-60). "12 days", "4 of 5", "42 units, range 30 to 60".

His data only.

A member sees his own data. Never a comparison with other members, an average, a percentile or "most men".

His answers are his answers.

Check-in answers are labelled as self-reported, on the scale he answered. Never framed as a clinical result, an improvement or a score.

Words before colour.

Every state is said in words and shown with an icon or a shape. Colour only repeats it. Each chart has a table or a sentence for screen readers.

No scary red.

Coral marks an out-of-range value only, with an icon and words (R-15). A day without a log is an empty dot, not a red one.

No games.

No streaks, scores, grades, badges or confetti. The portal informs; it never rewards or blames.

No data outside the portal.

Notifications, emails and lock screens never carry a number, a chart, a dose or a result. They say "You have an update" and link to sign-in.

A quiet palette from the brand tokens.

The brand colours are muted on purpose, so the chart palette is small: one ink for data, one hue ramp for amounts, two reserved status colours. Checked with the data-viz palette validator against Paper (16.11.2026).

Data ink.Most portal charts have one series. It is Deep Teal.
  • Series 1 · Deep Teal--color-brand-deep-teal · 9.82:1 on Paper
  • Series 2 · Pastel Blue--color-brand-blue · 3.53:1 · only beside Deep Teal, two series at most

Deep Teal and Pastel Blue stay apart under red-green colour blindness (difference 24, target 8). Both sit below the validator's colourfulness floor, which the brand accepts: a second series always carries a direct label and a second marker shape, so colour is never the only key.

Amounts: one hue, light to dark.Three steps for ordered or growing values. Passes the ordinal check: one hue, clear steps, the light end at 3.53:1.

--color-brand-blue → --color-brand-blue-dark → --color-brand-deep-teal. Wash Blue and Teal Deep are left out: Wash Blue is too faint for a mark (1.89:1) and Teal Deep is too close to Deep Teal to read as a step.

Status: reserved, with icon and words.Used only when a value has a range. Never for a series.
  • In range · Pastel Green--color-brand-green · 3.89:1 · check icon and "In range" (R-16)
  • Out of range · Coral--color-brand-coral · 3.01:1 · alert icon and words, never text colour (R-15)
  • Range band · Wash Green--color-surface-wash-green · a surface behind the marker; its edges are drawn in Slate
Structure, highlight and never.The frame stays quiet so the data can speak.
  • Gridline · Line Soft--color-line-soft · 1px, horizontal only
  • Baseline · Line Base--color-line-base · 1px
  • Track · Mist--color-surface-mist · the unfilled part of a ring or bar
  • Next step · Yellow--color-brand-yellow · once per screen, the next step on the plan, never a series (R-11)
  • Never · product, logo and wash coloursProduct colours (--color-product-*) name a product on packaging. They never encode data, not even for "your Tadalafil".
