Assessment.

The online assessment a man completes before a licensed provider reviews it, in one place: the patterns every screen follows, the question template and schema, the flow and its exits, every question for Sexual health at launch, each screen at 1440 and 390, the states, a working demo and the rules for the build.

Current · U-32

The flow, the patterns and the build rules are decided (U-32, Avi's decisions on the assessment screens). The clinical content is a draft: the clinical team writes and approves the final questions and the stop rules. Every question, threshold, branch and outcome on this page is DEMO ONLY (R-69). Times, partners, the charge line and legal lines are placeholders in square brackets until operations and legal confirm them (R-60, R-62, R-68). Nothing here is a diagnosis or a medical claim; a licensed provider reviews every assessment (R-64).

How it works.

Email first, then seven steps, one question per screen, then the provider. The exits are calm and say what to do next.

One question per screen.

A full question in DM Serif 26/1.3 (R-25), one helper line, the answers, Continue. A short follow-up opens on the same screen; a longer branch adds screens.

The capsule shows the way.

The progress mark is the logo capsule with its divider (R-42, R-109), in the content under the top bar. The step label sits beside it, never inside, and the fill is visible from the first question. Desktop: a rail lists the seven steps. Mobile: tapping the capsule opens the step list (U-32).

The line on every screen.

"Assessment only. Not a diagnosis." under the main button on every screen, desktop and mobile (R-63). The emergency line sits in every footer.

Saved as he goes.

The email comes first, so each answer saves on Continue. "Save and finish later" only sends a neutral link that signs him in, with no password; coming back opens the next open question (U-32).

No logic we invent.

Stop rules on this page are suggestions for the clinical team, marked as such. No thresholds, no eligibility promises, no product before the provider (R-69).

No health answer leaves the intake.

The assessment runs on the clinical intake domain with no pixels or session replay (R-70, R-71). Analytics run only after he accepts them in the consent banner, and then get screen IDs, never answers (U-31).

Flow patterns.

Branching.

A follow-up opens on the same screen when it is one short question; a longer branch adds screens and the step's share of the capsule updates. Branches live in the schema, never in the screen design.

Section break.

Between steps, one screen with a single sentence ("Next: your health history.") and Continue. No progress lost, no new promise.

Consent and privacy.

Before the first question: what happens to the answers and who reads them, plus the analytics consent banner. The consents for care (telehealth, Privacy notice and Terms) come in step 6, one checkbox each, never pre-ticked (U-32).

Save and resume.

The email comes first; every answer saves on Continue. Leaving shows "Leave this page." only when an answer is not saved yet. Coming back through the emailed link signs him in and opens the next open question.

Check and send.

The last screen: every answer with an Edit link that opens that one question and comes back. "Send to my provider" is the one Yellow button of the assessment.

Errors.

Inline, under the answers, in plain words, as a direct instruction with no blame (R-56). Continue stays active and explains. Network errors: the error toast, answers kept.

Mobile.

Continue in a bottom bar.

The Mobile bar button (48, full width, E-03) stays above the keyboard, with the compliance line under it.

The right keyboard.

Numeric for numbers and dates, email for email, tel for the phone; nothing zooms (inputs at 16px or more).

The step list on tap.

The capsule row is one 44px button; the list opens as a bottom sheet and closes with the close button, Esc or a tap outside.

The same question size.

Questions stay at 26/1.3 on a phone and wrap to up to four lines (R-25).

Question schema.

      Tap targets and keyboard.

      Every control at least 44 × 44 (R-74): cards are whole-row labels, text buttons are 44 tall, the Back icon is 44 × 44. Tab order follows the screen; radios move with the arrow keys; Enter on a field means Continue. Esc closes the save dialog and returns focus to its trigger.

      Step change and focus.

      On Continue, the next screen rises 12px in 220ms (R-53) and focus moves to the question (a heading with tabindex="-1"). A polite live region says "Step 3 of 7, Medications". With reduced motion the screen swaps at once and the capsule fills without a transition.

      Labels and errors.

      Each question is the group label (fieldset and legend, or role="radiogroup" with aria-labelledby). Helper and error lines are tied with aria-describedby. On an error: aria-invalid="true" on the field or the group, the message in a role="alert" line, focus to the first field with the error. Continue is never disabled for missing answers; it explains instead (pattern 2, U-32). Choices are checked on Continue; typed fields on blur after the first edit and on Continue; a message clears as soon as the answer is valid. The one disabled Continue for a stop is the state message (E-05, R-42), tied to the button with aria-describedby.

      Autosave and resume.

      The email is asked first (W2), so each answer saves to the intake server on Continue; long text also saves [after a short pause · engineering] while he types. A failed save keeps him on the question with the error toast and retries. Resume comes through a one-time sign-in link by email (no password, U-32) and opens the next unanswered question; changed questions are asked again. Saved answers are kept for [retention · legal]. Leaving with an unsaved answer shows "Leave this page." only then.

      Privacy.

      Answers live only on the clinical intake domain (R-70), encrypted in transit and at rest, readable by the care team only [HIPAA set-up · legal, engineering]. No answer in the URL, in local storage, in page titles or in error logs. The email link holds a one-time token, never an answer; the subject line is neutral. Uploads are checked and stored apart from the answers.

      Analytics after consent only.

      No ad pixels, tag managers, chat widgets or session replay on any assessment screen (R-71). First-party analytics only, with the events below, and only after he accepts analytics in the consent banner, here as on the whole site (U-31). Declined or not answered: nothing is sent. Legal may approve an exception later. The demo's event panel shows exactly what may be sent.
