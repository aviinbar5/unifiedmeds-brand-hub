Label anatomy.

Every label is an instance of the master (Figma 2680:16). Change structure, type and logo there, never on a single label.

  1. Ground. The category colour: Sexual health Deep Teal #264653, Longevity Forest #2F5240, Hair loss Sky #BFD9EA (a light label: text and seal in Deep Teal).
  2. Seal. The primary seal, small, reversed, top left.
  3. Product name. DM Serif Display in Paper.
  4. Strength · form. Strength in Inter SemiBold, form in Inter Regular, a middle dot between.
  5. Divider. A thin Paper line under the name block.
  6. Rx lines. "Prescription treatment" and "Rx only", Inter Regular.
  7. Capsule. One vertical capsule cropped by the right edge, outlined in the product colour, its upper half filled.

The label family.

Avi's label (Figma 2680:38, Product colours option B) kept exactly, and extended to every product. Approved (R-85, R-89). Four rules: the ground is the category; the capsule colour is the product; a capsule hue never repeats in the catalogue (hue family, not only the hex code); one product-name size per category (84, 79, 68). Unknown strengths read "[DOSE]" until the clinical team confirms them.

Rules.

Discretion rules for every outer surface.

Nothing outside says what it treats.

No symptom words, no anatomy, no "men's" anything on any outer surface.

Current · U-29The outside carries one line.

"Packed with care, just for you.", printed with a small outline capsule before it, and the sender name unifiedmeds. Nothing else: no product, no condition. The bottle inside carries the brand. Only the carrier checks stay with operations. See Unboxing.

No loose pills.

Tablet renders made for the Combo concept (LP 01, stage 3) show loose tablets and stay out of brand imagery (R-38).

Left behind.

Sachets, pouches and a lab kit from the earlier catalogue are not in the unifiedmeds catalogue.
