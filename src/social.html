Shared social rules.

They sit on top of the rules on Voice and compliance and the paid rules on the Ads page, which apply to every boosted or ad-ready post.

Two options, one logic.

Every asset comes as Option A (calm, editorial: a photo and a serif line) and Option B (graphic: the capsule kit and colour). Avi picks per asset; once picked, the other is archived.

Phone first.

Most people meet every channel on a phone. Each asset is checked at 390 wide: type stays readable (serif from about 21 px on screen, Inter from about 11 px), nothing important sits in the platform's own overlays.

One profile picture everywhere.

The same mark on every channel, from the shared pair below. Never the full seal or the wordmark at avatar size.

Safe zones before style.

Type and logo live inside the safe zone of each platform. A capsule may be cropped by the edge; if it would land under a platform button, it moves.

The legal line where an assessment is offered.

"Assessment only. Not a diagnosis." in the art of every ad and on the last card of a carousel that offers the assessment, and in the caption of every organic post that links to it (R-63).

Yellow once, for the action.

At most one Yellow element per asset, only for "Get started" (R-11, R-55). Organic posts usually have none.

Two capsule shapes per surface at most.

Type never sits on a shape, and a face is never covered by type, a card or a shape (R-106, R-110).

No claims, no medicine names, no proof.

No benefit claims, statistics, prices, reviews, before and after, or prescription names in any social asset or caption (R-58, R-65, R-67).

Discretion in public.

No condition or symptom hashtags. Never ask about health in comments; move to a private message, then to the assessment. Never tag or name a member.

Captions and alt text, always.

Video carries captions for every spoken word; every image has alt text that describes it plainly.

One profile picture for every channel.

The app icon's "u|m" capsule, from the logo file, never redrawn. Option A puts it in Navy on Cream; Option B is the app icon itself, White on Navy (R-93). Every platform masks it to a circle, so nothing sits in the corners. Master files 800 × 800; each kit exports its own size.

Current

Option B, the app icon on Navy, is the primary profile picture on all six channels (R-93, U-27). Option A, the Cream mark, stays on file as an alternative and is not used on a live profile. The Primary seal is not used at avatar size, since its words cannot be read in a 32 px circle.
