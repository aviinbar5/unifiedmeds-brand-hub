02 · Who we are

A men's health brand that starts with Sexual health.

One paragraph of positioning, the audience and the vibe. The boilerplates for the press are in the Media kit.

unifiedmeds is a men's health telehealth brand. It launches with Sexual health and will grow into further categories, starting with Hair loss and Longevity. A member completes an online assessment, a licensed provider reviews it and decides what happens next, and treatment arrives in closed, branded packaging. The brand speaks plainly and privately. It treats men's health as ordinary health care: no shame, no jokes, and no promises it cannot keep.

Audience

  • Men are the audience today. Hero and product photography leads with men aged 35 to 65 (R-37).
  • Partners appear in Sexual health stories, because the topic is shared.
  • Service and support sections (care team, delivery, member support) show a balanced mix of people of all genders (R-37, U-27).
  • Women: some planned products will also serve women Planned. The brand stays the same for them; only the casting changes.

Vibe

Masculine, on the gentle side. The brand has the calm of a man who knows what he wants and does not need to raise his voice. It is assured without being hard, and warm without being soft. The five traits below turn that into decisions.

03 · Personas

Three men, one brand.

Current The three personas are the audience reference for unifiedmeds (decided by Avi, U-31). Partners appear in their stories, as photography allows in Sexual health (R-37).

The Deferrer · 46 to 58

Something has been off for years; he is not in crisis, so he has done nothing. Wants permission to take it seriously without making it a whole thing.

The Optimiser · 32 to 41

Tracks sleep and weight already. Wants raw information and someone qualified to read it, not a black box.

The Prompted · 55 to 68

Here because a partner asked. Drops out at the first friction or embarrassment. Wants a short, clear path with a person at the end.

Ages describe the audience, not the casting. The Optimiser starts at 32 and the Prompted runs to 68, outside the 35 to 65 casting for hero and product photography (R-37): photography keeps 35 to 65. On a phone the three cards stack in this order.

Five traits, three principles.

The traits say how the brand behaves; the principles say what it puts first. Both turn the vibe into decisions.

Principles

Human first, clinical second.

Lead with the sentence he would say out loud. Follow with the one a clinician would sign.

Discreet by design.

Privacy shows in the layout and the packaging, not only in the policy. No shame cues, no bodies as proof.

Mature, never macho.

Confidence comes from whitespace and specificity. No flames, wolves or “alpha”.

Insights

What the brand never does

  • Makes benefit or medical claims, or promises a result.
  • Shows clinical statistics, press logos, reviews or star ratings before legal approves real data.
  • Uses shame, fear, innuendo or jokes about performance.
  • Names a prescription drug in a hero, an ad or next to a benefit.
  • Presents an assessment as a diagnosis.
  • Shows before/after images, loose pills or clinic scenes.
  • Collects health data on the marketing site, or runs tracking pixels on condition and product pages.

05 · Name

The name is unifiedmeds.

One spelling everywhere. Rule R-07, decision U-01. How products, categories and plans are named: Architecture and naming.

Write it unifiedmeds.

All lowercase, one word: in running text, headings, legal copy and alt text, also at the start of a sentence. The legal entity name is still to confirm (O-01).

Never Unifiedmeds, UnifiedMeds, Unified Meds, unified meds or UNIFIEDMEDS, and never “UM” as a short name.

“UM” as letters never stands for the company, in copy or as a logo. That is not the u|m mark of the app icon, which is part of the logo (R-93, R-108). Any “Unifiedmeds” left in Figma copy changes to “unifiedmeds”. The former brand name never appears in new material.

08 · Shapes

Two shape families: the Quadrant Kit and the capsule.

Every graphic shape in the brand comes from the six Quadrant Kit pieces or from the capsule of the logo, and nothing else.

The capsule

The capsule comes from the logo and is the second shape family. Figma "Capsule Kit" (Packaging 2678:2) has four variants. On the website it may be filled, outline or transparent, shown whole or cropped by the frame edge, and it may crop a photo. Rules R-31 to R-33, decision U-03.

Current · R-105

Every drawn capsule has the logo's proportions. It is not symmetric: the left half is longer (55% of the length) and the right half is taller (the left half is 93.3% of its height). Standing, it is the same capsule turned 90° clockwise, long half on top. Draw it with tools/capsule_svg.py or use the files in assets/patterns/; never a rounded rectangle or a --radius-pill box with equal halves.

Shown in website colours, stroke 0.85% of the length (R-109). Files: one set in website colours, lying and standing, as outline, split and solid, in assets/patterns/. The earlier Sky and Logo Blue set leaves for the archive: logo colours stay on the logo (R-84, U-31).

Current · R-109

The stroke scales with the capsule. An outline capsule's stroke is 0.85% of its full length, as in the seal, never less than 1.5px (--size-capsule-stroke-ratio, --size-capsule-stroke-min). At every size the capsule keeps the divider, like the seal (R-109): the long half is drawn open toward the joint, the short, taller half is closed by the divider line, and the step shows where the two heights meet. Never one continuous outline without the divider. A large capsule that needs weight takes a fill, solid or translucent, never a thicker stroke. The fixed 20px stroke (--size-capsule-stroke) is retired.

Stroke scale, at true size.

  • 64px · stroke 1.5px · dividerTwo halves and the divider, like the seal. Stroke at the 1.5px minimum.

  • 96px · stroke 1.5px · divider0.85% would be 0.8px, so the 1.5px minimum applies.

  • 120px · stroke 1.5px · dividerThe same construction as the seal: divider and step. 1.5px.

  • 240px · stroke 2px · dividerStroke 2px (0.85% of 240). The divider stays.

  • 360px · stroke 3.1px · dividerStroke 3.1px. The divider and the step between the halves read clearly.

  • 600px · stroke 5.1px · dividerStroke 5.1px: the page-head capsule of this hub, divider included.

  • Don't · 360px · fixed 20px strokeA thick fixed stroke swallows the 7px step between the halves: the capsule reads as a symmetric pill.

Drawn by tools/capsule_svg.py (stroke_for(), capsule_paths(), svg_outline()). The stroke follows the length as rendered: a capsule scaled down on a phone keeps its proportion, and below 176px long the 1.5px minimum takes over.

Rules for both families

  1. Two families only. Quadrant Kit pieces and the capsule. No oval, no soft-cornered rectangle, no freehand curve.
  2. Use them as needed. Either family, or both together, on any website surface (U-03).
  3. No text on a shape. Never a headline, a label or a disclaimer on top of a shape. A photo may be cropped to a capsule.
  4. Capsule finishes. Filled, outline or transparent; whole or cropped by the frame edge.
  5. Quadrant pieces stay axis-aligned. At rest every piece sits square to the frame. A piece may rotate only during its entrance (Pivot).
  6. Enters from a corner toward the subject. The curve faces the content, so the shape frames it rather than pushing it away.
  7. Two shapes per surface at most. Any mix of capsule and Quadrant Kit pieces counts; a cropped repeat of the same capsule counts as one; a pattern counts as one and leaves no room for a second (R-106). Opacity 70% for outlines (--opacity-capsule) and 50% for fills (--opacity-capsule-soft), R-32.

Do and don't

Shapes in folds.

Current · R-111

Up to two shapes a fold, and a shape may cross into the next. Each fold carries at most two shapes (R-106): the capsule in its true shape or Quadrant Kit pieces, outline or translucent fill, at --opacity-capsule or --opacity-capsule-soft, never under text. A shape may straddle the boundary between two folds; it counts once in each and carries the eye down the page. Optional scroll motion: the shape drifts at most 24px (--motion-distance-drift) or turns at most 6° (--motion-drift-rotate) across the fold's scroll, or draws its outline once. Only shapes move, never text. Reduced motion: the shape rests at its end state.

One accent colour per section.

Current · R-115

A section may add one brand accent. Green or Blue, through an icon, an eyebrow mark, a rule or a low-opacity shape of the same hue. One accent per section, never both. Yellow stays the single result moment. Text keeps its own colours: Green and Blue fall under 4.5:1 on Paper, so they mark, they never carry small text.

Mobile: the same section stacks; the shape keeps its corner and shrinks with the card, the icon stays 40px.

Entrance

The piece arrives, then stays still.

Pivot: the quarter turns 90° up from its corner, toward the copy. --motion-duration-shape 520 ms, --motion-easing-enter, no fade. Plays once; with reduced motion it appears in place. Full set on Motion.
