The emails the service sends on its own: order, provider reply, shipping and refill. Discreet first: nothing in a subject line, preview or body says what a member is treated for. Every line waits for legal and operations.

Rules.

The inbox is not private.

Subject, preview text and body never name a condition, a medicine, a symptom or a result. Details stay behind sign-in.

One email, one job.

One headline, one or two lines, one button. Tell him what happened and what comes next.

Sender: unifiedmeds.

From name "unifiedmeds", from address on the brand domain (O-01). Replies reach a person.

The provider decides.

Every email that touches treatment says a licensed provider reviews it; nothing renews or ships without that review.

No pressure.

No countdowns, discounts, "last chance" or exclamation marks in system emails (R-61, R-62).

No health data by email.

No answers, notes, prescriptions or lab values in an email, ever. The account holds them.

Templates.

True email HTML at 600 wide. Open one on its own, or download it as a starting point for the email platform. Subject lines follow the same rules as the newsletter subject bank: no condition, medicine or result.
