Logo stings.

Three approved versions (R-101). Each is 8 seconds, 1920 × 1080, MP4 with sound, and all three end on the same frame: the capsule seal on Cream, the logo presentation ground (--color-logo-cream, R-84). B is the default, C1 is for brand and story moments, C2 for short social openers. Use one sting per video, at the start or the end, never in the middle.

B · Two halves.

The capsule seal builds and settles on Ink, then Cream opens out of it.

File: unifiedmeds-logo-sting-B.mp4 (3.3 MB). The current render, with the true capsule (R-105).

C1 · From u.

One letter builds the whole mark. The bold u turns 90 degrees and stretches into the open half; its mirror swings out from the seam and becomes the closed half.

File: unifiedmeds-logo-sting-C1-u.mp4 (3.8 MB). Built from the same geometry as B.

C2 · From u + m.

"um" appears with the weights of the wordmark: a bold u, as in "unified", and a light m, as in "meds". Both letters turn together into the two halves of the capsule.

File: unifiedmeds-logo-sting-C2-um.mp4 (4.0 MB). The m in the opening is drawn geometrically, close to the wordmark's m but not the font glyph itself.
