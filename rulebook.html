  • R-109 new Every capsule keeps the logo's construction at every size, as in the seal: the left (long) half is drawn open toward the joint, the right (short, taller) half is closed by the divider line, and the step shows where the two heights meet. A filled long half sits inside the outline with a gap, like the seal's filled half. A large capsule that needs weight takes a fill, never a thicker stroke. Draw it with tools/capsule_svg.py (capsule_paths(), svg_outline(), stroke_for()) or the .um-cap masks. Motion

  • R-49 same One principle: "Motion confirms, it never performs."
  • R-50 same Durations: 150 hover, press, select, focus · 220 open, close, slide, accordion, step · 320 reveal · 420 wipe · 520 shape · 1400 loader. No other duration.
