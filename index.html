How this hub works.

Three sources, one order. When two sources disagree, the higher one wins and the lower one gets fixed.

  1. Figma wins on every visual value.The website file h7D0eRszARBaWdJT1NWrpZ defines layout, colour, type, spacing, radius and components. The logo file VX5oKsl1ZIKbiWLR0tjf7L, page "B · capsule seal", defines the logo.
  2. brand-rules.md wins on compliance and wording.The rulebook holds the rules, the decision log, the exceptions log and the open items.
  3. The hub explains and mirrors both.Values come from tokens/tokens.json, generated into brand.css. If the hub and Figma disagree, the hub is wrong.

What changed in Edition 03.

Edition 03 is the first unifiedmeds edition, built on Avi's ten decisions of 15.11.2026.

  • The name is unifiedmeds, all lowercase, everywhere.
  • The capsule seal is the only brand symbol. Logo colours are their own group, used by the logo only.
  • The website palette, type, spacing, buttons and motion stay as in Edition 02.
  • The capsule joins the Quadrant Kit as a brand shape: filled, outline or transparent, whole or cropped.
  • Launch is Sexual health with Tadalafil, Sildenafil and Combo. Longevity and Hair loss follow in Phase 2.
  • New: a logo chapter, a packaging chapter, product colours and a line icon set built on the capsule.
  • Earlier editions are archived outside the published hub. Nothing was deleted.
