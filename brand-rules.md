### Brand and naming

- **R-05** [changed] unifiedmeds is a men's health telehealth brand that launches with Sexual health. Vibe: masculine, on the gentle side. · Source: U-04
- **R-06** [changed] Launch catalogue: Tadalafil, Sildenafil and Combo (confirmed by Avi 17.11.2026, U-21). Longevity and Hair loss are Phase 2 categories (designed in Figma, not linked as live at launch). Some future products will be for women (Planned). · Source: U-04, U-05

Never "Unifiedmeds", "UnifiedMeds", "Unified Meds" or "UNIFIEDMEDS", and never the former brand name. The legal entity name is **To confirm**; until then no entity name appears in new material, and the former company name never appears. · Source: U-01, U-07, U-31
- **R-08** [same] The category is "Sexual health" in navigation and on the category page. "Better Intimacy" is used only as the editorial hero title.
- **R-108** [new] Product names say what the product is. A single medicine is named by the medicine, then strength and form ("Tadalafil, 20 mg tablets"). A combination gets a short descriptive name ("Combo") with its ingredients written under it. Never a reference brand ("Generic Cialis", "like Viagra"), never a word of result or speed, anatomy or the condition. "UM" is never a short name for the company. In running text a generic name is lower case mid-sentence (tadalafil); on labels the strength follows R-89 (20 mg · Tablets). Product names never appear in email subjects, previews, push notifications or on the mailer. New names follow the naming process on the Brand architecture page (brief, long list, screen, trademark and FDA check, legal, Avi decides). · Source: U-21
- **R-09** [same] A tagline sits under the name. Until Avi chooses one, no tagline ships. **To confirm.**

### Shape and imagery

- **R-30** [changed] Radius: buttons 5 (`--radius-button`); cards 16; fields, chips and the dropdown panel 12; menu rows and thumbnails 8; checkboxes 5, like buttons; chips, nav pills and status pills 999 (`--radius-pill`). No other radius. A checkbox is 22px with a 1.5px Line/Field border (components.css `.check`). The capsule is a shape (R-31), not a radius value. · Source: U-11, Figma 2676:310
- **R-31** [changed] Two shape families. **Quadrant Kit:** Square, Quarter, Half, Circle, Arch, Arc. **Capsule:** the logo's capsule form, in the variants Split, Split Blue, Outline and Vertical (Figma "Capsule Kit", 2678:2), plus the capsule as an image crop. Both families may appear on the website, as needed. No oval and no soft-cornered rectangle. The capsule shape files are one set in website colours: `assets/shapes/capsule-{outline,split,solid}-{h,v}.svg` plus `capsule-loader.svg`; the Sky / Logo Blue set is archived (R-84). · Source: U-03, U-31
- **R-32** [changed] A capsule may be filled, outline or transparent, and may be shown whole or cropped by the frame edge. Website pattern (Figma homepage, Avi 15.11.2026): a large outline capsule in Wash Blue at 70% opacity (`--opacity-capsule`), cropped by a section edge; vertical in dark sections; filled capsules at 50% (`--opacity-capsule-soft`). Quadrant pieces stay axis-aligned and may rotate only during their entrance. How many shapes per surface: R-106. The stroke follows R-109: it scales with the length, never a fixed width. · Source: U-03, Figma 2673:209
- **R-33** [changed] No text on a shape, ever. A disclaimer never sits on a shape. A photo may be cropped to a capsule.
- **R-105** [new] Every drawn capsule has the logo's proportions; it is never symmetric. The left half is longer (55% of the full length) and the right half is taller (the left half is 93.3% of its height), measured from `assets/brand/logo-seal.svg`. A standing capsule is the same shape turned 90° clockwise, long half on top. This applies to every capsule that stands for the logo or the Capsule Kit: split, outline, filled, cropped decor, bands, loaders, icons, illustrations, packaging art, motion and "don't" examples. Draw it only with `tools/capsule_svg.py` (SVG) or the `.um-cap` masks from `assets/shapes/` (outline and split, at 176px length or more); never a rounded rectangle, `border-radius: 999px` or `roundRect`, and size it by one side only so the ratio cannot stretch. Generic UI pills (chips, status, toggles) are not the capsule and keep R-30. · Source: U-22
- **R-106** [new] Two shapes per surface at most (a slide, a post, a section, a card, an ad): any mix of Capsule and Quadrant Kit pieces counts. A cropped repeat of the same capsule counts as one. Patterns count as one shape and leave no room for a second. · Source: U-23, closes O-05
- **R-109** [new] Every capsule keeps the logo's construction at every size, as in the seal: the left (long) half is drawn open toward the joint, the right (short, taller) half is closed by the divider line, and the step shows where the two heights meet. Never one continuous outline without the divider (Avi, 17.11.2026: "like the original logo"). The stroke scales with the length: 0.85% of the full length, never under 1.5px (`--size-capsule-stroke-ratio`, `--size-capsule-stroke-min`). A filled long half sits inside the outline with a gap, like the seal's filled half. A large capsule that needs weight takes a fill, never a thicker stroke. The stroke applies as rendered to icons, spots and patterns (U-31): spots have one file per size (160 and 96), patterns are used at 1x. Draw it with `tools/capsule_svg.py` (`capsule_paths()`, `svg_outline()`, `stroke_for()`) or the `.um-cap` masks from `assets/shapes/` (outline and split, at 176px length or more). A fully filled capsule keeps a thin gap at the divider (one stroke wide), like the seal. · Source: U-25, U-26, U-28, U-31
- **R-110** [new] On a photo the capsule is an outline (R-109), Paper on dark or busy photos and Deep Teal on light ones, with its long half filled translucent (Paper or Wash Blue at `--opacity-capsule-soft`). It enters from a frame edge or corner, cropped by it, toward the subject; never over a face, eyes or mouth, never under text, never an opaque fill. At most two shapes per photo, the logo icon included (R-106). The logo icon stamp is the u|m mark from the app icon without its tile (`assets/shapes/logo-stamp-paper.svg`, `logo-stamp-teal.svg`), long side 48px on desktop and 40 on mobile, 16px from the edges; in exported images 12% of the short side, set 1.5% of the short side from the edges (16px on 1080). On mobile the shape scales with the photo; if the crop would put it over a face it moves to another edge or hides. · Source: U-25
- **R-111** [new] A fold (section) carries up to two shapes (R-106): the true capsule or Quadrant Kit pieces, outline or translucent fill (`--opacity-capsule`, `--opacity-capsule-soft`), never under text. A shape may straddle the boundary between two folds, creating continuity down the page; it counts once in each fold. Optional scroll-linked motion: drift at most 24px (`--motion-distance-drift`) or turn at most 6° (`--motion-drift-rotate`), or draw the outline once. Only shapes move, never text. Reduced motion: the end state, static. This extends R-53 and R-104 to every page. · Source: U-25
- **R-34** [changed] Logo: see R-90 to R-94. The former brand's quadrant symbol is Retired and kept only in the archive.
- **R-35** [changed] Icons: the unifiedmeds line set built on the capsule (12 feature icons, Figma 3042:221): 1.5px stroke, round caps, no fill, drawn on a 64px grid. Icons are Deep Teal, never Navy and never Yellow; on Deep Teal grounds only the container variant or a Paper line (U-27). The capsule icon family (`assets/icons-capsule/`, lying and standing) is the website's working set for rows, cards and menus; the feature icons stay for larger moments. Placed sizes: capsule icons 48×24 and 40×20 lying, 64 and 48 standing; feature icons 64 and 48; UI glyphs (`assets/icons/ui/`) are drawn on a 20 grid at 1.75 and placed at 20, or 24 in icon-only controls. The 1.5px floor applies to the line as rendered: one exported file per placed size (`assets/icons/sized/`, `assets/icons-capsule/sized/`), never a scaled file (U-31). The only open point is A or B per subject. · Source: Figma 3042:221, U-27, U-31
- **R-36** [same] The UI is flat, with borders. One shadow, for dropdowns and dialogs only.
- **R-37** [changed] Photography: men aged 35 to 65 lead the brand's hero and product imagery. Partners may appear in sexual-health stories. Service and support sections (care team, delivery, member support) use a balanced, modular mix of people of all genders (Avi, 17.11.2026, U-27). Women lead only in future women's products.
- **R-38** [same] Closed, branded bottles are allowed. Never loose pills, syringes, lab coats or before/after images. At most one faceless detail crop per page.
- **R-39** [changed] Watermarked placeholder photos never ship on the website, ads or press. Exception (U-27): inside the hub a watermarked paid preview may stand in, with a small "Buy this image" link and the tag "Not approved · to replace". The live site uses a commissioned shoot for the key images (home, the three category heroes, about) and licensed stock with model release and sensitive-use clearance for the rest (U-32, O-13 closed). · Source: U-27, U-31, U-32
- **R-120** [new] Copy and photos work three ways: beside the photo, on a solid colour panel, or directly on the photo. The panel is Paper, Deep Teal or Mist, radius 8, inset 16. Directly on the photo only on a calm area (a wall, a window, sky, a floor), never across a face, skin or a shape (R-33, R-110). Contrast is measured on the area under the letters at every crop: 4.5:1 under 24px, 3:1 for DM Serif at 24px and up; Teal Deep or Deep Teal on light areas, Paper on dark ones; no scrim, gradient or blur. On a photo a headline and one short line at most, and buttons are Outline Light. On a phone, if the crop loses the calm area, the copy moves below the photo. · Source: U-31
