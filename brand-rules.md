## Rules

### Authority

- **R-01** [changed] Figma `h7D0eRszARBaWdJT1NWrpZ` (the website file, still named "SoviCare - Website" in Figma) wins on every visual value. The logo file `VX5oKsl1ZIKbiWLR0tjf7L`, page "B · capsule seal", wins on the logo. If the hub and Figma disagree, the hub is wrong and the hub is fixed.

- **R-04** [changed] This is Brand Edition 03 (15.11.2026), the first unifiedmeds edition. SoviCare Editions 01 and 02 are archived, never deleted and never linked as current.

### Brand and naming

- **R-05** [changed] unifiedmeds is a men's health telehealth brand that launches with Sexual health. Vibe: masculine, on the gentle side. · Source: U-04
- **R-06** [changed] Launch catalogue: Tadalafil, Sildenafil and Combo (confirmed by Avi 17.11.2026, U-21). Longevity and Hair loss are Phase 2 categories (designed in Figma, not linked as live at launch). Some future products will be for women (Planned). · Source: U-04, U-05

Product names never appear in email subjects, previews, push notifications or on the mailer. New names follow the naming process on the extended hub page Brand architecture (brief, long list, screen, trademark and FDA check, legal, Avi decides). · Source: U-21

### Shape and imagery

- **R-30** [changed] Radius: buttons 5 (`--radius-button`); cards 16; fields, chips and the dropdown panel 12; menu rows and thumbnails 8; chips, nav pills and status pills 999 (`--radius-pill`). No other radius. The capsule is a shape (R-31), not a radius value. · Source: U-11, Figma 2676:310
- **R-31** [changed] Two shape families. **Quadrant Kit:** Square, Quarter, Half, Circle, Arch, Arc. **Capsule:** the logo's capsule form, in the variants Split, Split Blue, Outline and Vertical (Figma "Capsule Kit", 2678:2), plus the capsule as an image crop. Both families may appear on the website, as needed. No oval and no soft-cornered rectangle. · Source: U-03
- **R-32** [changed] A capsule may be filled, outline or transparent, and may be shown whole or cropped by the frame edge. Website pattern (Figma homepage, Avi 15.11.2026): a large outline capsule with a 20px stroke (`--size-capsule-stroke`) in Wash Blue at 70% opacity (`--opacity-capsule`), cropped by a section edge; vertical in dark sections; filled capsules at 50% (`--opacity-capsule-soft`). Quadrant pieces stay axis-aligned and may rotate only during their entrance. How many shapes per surface: R-106. The stroke follows R-109 (the 20px stroke is retired). · Source: U-03, Figma 2673:209
- **R-33** [changed] No text on a shape, ever. A disclaimer never sits on a shape. A photo may be cropped to a capsule.
- **R-105** [new] Every drawn capsule has the logo's proportions; it is never symmetric. The left half is longer (55% of the full length) and the right half is taller (the left half is 93.3% of its height), measured from `assets/brand/logo-seal.svg`. A standing capsule is the same shape turned 90° clockwise, long half on top. This applies to every capsule that stands for the logo or the Capsule Kit: split, outline, filled, cropped decor, bands, loaders, icons, illustrations, packaging art, motion and "don't" examples. Draw it only with `tools/capsule_svg.py` (SVG) or the `.um-cap` mask classes (extended hub); never a rounded rectangle, `border-radius: 999px` or `roundRect`, and size it by one side only so the ratio cannot stretch. Generic UI pills (chips, status, toggles) are not the capsule and keep R-30. · Source: U-22
- **R-106** [new] Two shapes per surface at most (a slide, a post, a section, a card, an ad): any mix of Capsule and Quadrant Kit pieces counts. A cropped repeat of the same capsule counts as one. Patterns count as one shape and leave no room for a second. · Source: U-23, closes O-05
- **R-109** [new] Every capsule keeps the logo's construction at every size, as in the seal: the left (long) half is drawn open toward the joint, the right (short, taller) half is closed by the divider line, and the step shows where the two heights meet. Never one continuous outline without the divider (Avi, 17.11.2026: "like the original logo"). The stroke scales with the length: 0.85% of the full length, never under 1.5px (`--size-capsule-stroke-ratio`, `--size-capsule-stroke-min`). A filled long half sits inside the outline with a gap, like the seal's filled half. A large capsule that needs weight takes a fill, never a thicker stroke. Draw it with `tools/capsule_svg.py` (`capsule_paths()`, `svg_outline()`, `stroke_for()`) or the `.um-cap` masks. A fully filled capsule keeps a thin gap at the divider (one stroke wide), like the seal. · Source: U-25, U-26, U-28
- **R-110** [new] On a photo the capsule is an outline (R-109), Paper on dark or busy photos and Deep Teal on light ones, with its long half filled translucent (Paper or Wash Blue at `--opacity-capsule-soft`). It enters from a frame edge or corner, cropped by it, toward the subject; never over a face, eyes or mouth, never under text, never an opaque fill. At most two shapes per photo, the logo icon included (R-106). The logo icon stamp is the u|m mark from the app icon without its tile (`assets/shapes/logo-stamp-paper.svg`, `logo-stamp-teal.svg`), long side 48px on desktop and 40 on mobile, 16px from the edges; in exported images 12% of the short side. On mobile the shape scales with the photo; if the crop would put it over a face it moves to another edge or hides. · Source: U-25
- **R-111** [new] A fold (section) carries up to two shapes (R-106): the true capsule or Quadrant Kit pieces, outline or translucent fill (`--opacity-capsule`, `--opacity-capsule-soft`), never under text. A shape may straddle the boundary between two folds, creating continuity down the page; it counts once in each fold. Optional scroll-linked motion: drift at most 24px (`--motion-distance-drift`) or turn at most 6° (`--motion-drift-rotate`), or draw the outline once. Only shapes move, never text. Reduced motion: the end state, static. This extends R-53 and R-104 to every page. · Source: U-25
- **R-34** [changed] Logo: see R-90 to R-94. The SoviCare quadrant symbol is Retired and kept only in the archive.
- **R-35** [changed] Icons: the unifiedmeds line set built on the capsule (12 icons, Figma 3042:221). 1.5px stroke, round caps, no fill, never Yellow. Drawn on a 64px grid. Placed sizes and icon colour on the website are **To confirm** (`GAP`: Figma places them at 40 and 48px; Foundations draws them in Navy, which R-84 reserves for the logo). · Source: Figma 3042:221 **Decided 17.11.2026 (U-27):** icons are Deep Teal (never Navy, never Yellow); the capsule icon family (horizontal and standing, `assets/icons-capsule/` in the extended hub) is the website's working set for rows, cards and menus, and the 12 drawn feature icons stay for larger moments; standing icons 64 on desktop, 48 on mobile; on Deep Teal grounds only the container variant or a Paper line.

- **R-37** [changed] Photography: men aged 35 to 65 lead the brand's hero and product imagery. Partners may appear in sexual-health stories. Service and support sections (care team, delivery, member support) use a balanced, modular mix of people of all genders (Avi, 17.11.2026, U-27). Women lead only in future women's products.
- **R-38** [same] Closed, branded bottles are allowed. Never loose pills, syringes, lab coats or before/after images. At most one faceless detail crop per page.
- **R-39** [changed] Watermarked placeholder photos never ship on the website, ads or press. ### Motion

- **R-49** [same] One principle: "Motion confirms, it never performs."
