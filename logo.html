Three lockups, one job each.

Pick the lockup by the space, not by taste. Source: Figma logo · Unifiedmeds (3038:178), rule R-90.

Navy on light, reversed on dark.

On Paper, Mist or Cream use the main version. On Deep Teal, Ink, Navy and on photography use the reversed version only. The website footer uses the horizontal reversed lockup on Deep Teal. Rule R-91.

Give the logo room.

Clear space is the height of the "meds" half on every side (x). Nothing enters that space: no text, no shape, no edge of the frame. Rule R-92.
