A few questions worth asking your provider.

Hi [FIRST_NAME],

A first conversation about your health can feel like a lot. It helps to arrive with a short list. Here are a few questions that make the conversation easier, whatever brings you in.

•What are my options, and how do they differ?
•What should I watch for, and when should I get in touch?
•How does this fit with anything else I take?
•What happens if my needs change?

There is no wrong question. Your provider is there to answer them, in plain words.
